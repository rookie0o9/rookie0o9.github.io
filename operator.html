FIXAM // OPERATOR PROFILE

Operator

I work at the intersection of support, infrastructure, identity, and security operations. My background is rooted in real environments: keeping systems running, reducing operational risk, hardening access, and dealing with the messy gap between how platforms are designed and how organisations actually use them.

This site documents that work in public. Some posts are incident-minded. Some are repair logs. Some are build notes from labs and delivery projects. The common thread is simple: practical systems thinking, clear engineering decisions, and lessons worth writing down.

active trajectory

from operations to engineering

Most of my work starts with one of three things: an operational problem, a security concern, or a system behaving strangely enough to deserve a closer look. I am interested in the point where administration becomes engineering, where repeated issues turn into patterns, patterns turn into controls, and controls become part of a more reliable platform.

secure delivery focus

Lately that means going deeper into secure delivery and cloud infrastructure: Azure, Terraform, Azure DevOps, workload identity, policy-minded design, and the foundational decisions that make automation safer and easier to scale.

security + operability

I am especially drawn to work that improves both security and operability at the same time: better identity boundaries, cleaner delivery pipelines, more defensible defaults, and clearer operational runbooks.

current direction

Right now, I am building toward a stronger DevOps and security engineering profile through hands-on delivery, not just study. The goal is to move from being someone who supports and secures systems to someone who can also shape how those systems are built, deployed, and governed.

areas i work across

identity and access controls endpoint and user security microsoft 365 and azure administration infrastructure troubleshooting and operational hygiene terraform and azure devops delivery foundations governance-minded lab work in azure ai foundry

If it involves secure access, strange failures, repeatable delivery, or practical infrastructure lessons, it probably belongs here.
